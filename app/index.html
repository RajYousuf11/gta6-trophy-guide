Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough, 4/10 difficulty

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, 60–80 hours. Finish all five chapters and the final mission. Do optional missions as they appear, scan new vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you go — that progress already counts toward 100%. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see Positive Mental Attitude): after Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. It is the only possibly missable trophy moment in the game.
  2. Step 2 — 100% completion checklist, about 100 hours. After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The thresholds are forgiving — roughly 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, any 100 optional missions. A few things need everything: all robberies, all underwater discoveries, all shooting ranges, all drone races, all of Bret’s quests.
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup, about 10 hours. Finish 8 stranger mission strands, the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), then mop up the count trophies: 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK scans, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, money and garage trophies. The platinum pops when the last of the other 54 is earned.

No online trophies. No difficulty settings, no mission medals, no mission replay and no New Game Plus — so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off on your trophy save. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap, Exophase trophy list.
